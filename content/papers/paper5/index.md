##### Abstract

We develop parameterized generalized fourth-order decompositions (factorized generalized fourth-order decomposition) to replace the second-order Trotter splitting in imaginary-time path integral molecular dynamics, and optimize the parameters through analysis of the analytically solvable one-dimensional harmonic oscillator. The fourth-order decomposition methods and optimized parameters can be applied to finite-difference-based open-chain and closed-chain PIMD, effectively improving the sampling efficiency and enlarging the accessible systems.
