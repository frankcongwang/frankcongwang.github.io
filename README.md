# Cong Wang (王聪)

**Postdoctoral Researcher, School of Physics, Peking University**

I work on path integral molecular dynamics (PIMD) — developing algorithms that make quantum simulations of nuclei faster, and building a Lee–Yang theory framework for the fermion sign problem.

- 📧 Email: frankcongwang@pku.edu.cn
- 🏠 Website: [frankcongwang.github.io](https://frankcongwang.github.io/)
- 🎓 Ph.D. in Physical Chemistry, Beijing Normal University (2022), advisors: Jiu-Shu Shao, Jian Liu
- 🔬 Postdoc with Xin-Zheng Li, Peking University (2023–present)

## Research Interests

- **Acceleration of PIMD methods** — middle-scheme thermostat algorithms (adopted by Amber), factorized generalized fourth-order decompositions, and perturbed path integrals
- **Fermion sign problem & PIMD of indistinguishable particles** — Lee–Yang zeros, analytic continuation on the complex plane, and re-weighted simulation for interacting systems
